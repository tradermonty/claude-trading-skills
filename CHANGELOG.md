# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are recorded here in [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) format. Repository releases use stable [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) tags (`vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`). A `.skill` package does not have an independent version.

- pair-trade-screener: a z-score exactly at the entry threshold (`abs(z) == entry_zscore`) now takes the direction of its sign, so `+2.0` with the default entry reports SHORT SPREAD and `-2.0` reports LONG SPREAD in both the signal and the position-sizing blocks instead of falling through to the opposite leg. The sizing block prints fractional share counts to 2 decimals and states that the dollar amounts use the unrounded sizes, so the printed counts and notionals agree at the shown precision and the share ratio still matches beta (#909). Rebuilt `skill-packages/pair-trade-screener.skill`.
- pair-trade-screener: size `analyze_spread.py` legs in share space (`shares_a = allocation / (price_a + beta * price_b)`, `shares_b = beta * shares_a`) instead of multiplying leg-A dollar notional by the price-level OLS slope `beta`, keeping the share hedge ratio aligned with `spread = price_a - beta * price_b` and gross notional within the `$10,000` example allocation; omit leg sizing with an explicit message when `beta <= 0` or prices are invalid. Updated skill docs and rebuilt `skill-packages/pair-trade-screener.skill` (#899).

A partial run still reports the remaining indexes and exits 0. Rebuilt `skill-packages/ibd-distribution-day-monitor.skill` (Refs #707).

- exposure-coach: the ftd-detector IBD-style market status now caps the exposure ceiling as an overlay on the result, not as a weighted component, so the composite score and its old outputs stay comparable. `exposure_ceiling_pct = min(composite ceiling, cap)`: `market_in_correction` caps at 20% and lowers NEW_ENTRY_ALLOWED to REDUCE_ONLY (CASH_PRIORITY stays); `confirmed_uptrend` on an FTD caps at 40% under 5 sessions since the FTD, 60% for 5-14, 80% for 15 or more and 60% when the age is unknown; a confirmed uptrend resumed on a new high without an FTD caps at 80% and is labelled as such; `uptrend_under_pressure` is one 20-point band lower (minimum 20%) with a "be selective" rationale and `cycle.pressure_reasons`. A null or absent status, old ftd-detector output or no `--ftd` gives no overlay and no new keys. New `market_status_overlay` block (`status`, `legacy_state`, `cap_pct`, `applied`, `reason`, `days_since_ftd`, `per_index`, `pressure_reasons`), also in the Markdown report and rationale. The caps are uncalibrated, IBD-inspired model choices; IBD's own guidance is cited from secondary reports ([1](https://inkl.com/news/trump-tariff-sell-off-how-ibd-s-risk-management-rules-avoided-big-losses), [2](https://www.inkl.com/news/how-to-invest-after-a-follow-through-day-3-breakout-stocks-to-watch)). A maintainer test found that varying exposure by these signals barely changed index-level returns (S&P 500, Nasdaq, RSP, SPY, QQQ) versus holding the same average exposure; the ceiling judges whether the environment supports adding individual-stock positions and is not an index trading signal. Rebuilt `skill-packages/exposure-coach.skill`.
- ftd-detector, ibd-distribution-day-monitor: compare volume with the most recent full-length session on an early-close (half-day) session and on the session after it, resolved through the vendored XNYS calendar, so a structurally low half day no longer turns the next session into an automatic distribution day or suppresses a valid follow-through day. - ftd-detector: two tuning knobs make the distribution correction trigger stricter, both read from the environment when an index is analyzed (an invalid value warns on stderr and uses the default; effective values in `cycle.parameters`): `FTD_CORRECTION_DD_COUNT` (integer 6-10, default 6) and `FTD_CORRECTION_MIN_DRAWDOWN_PCT` (number 0-9.99, default 0 = none; when above 0 the close must also be at least that far below the post-confirmation peak close). The defaults keep the current behavior and are uncalibrated; the undercut and the 10% deep drawdown are unchanged. The drawdown comparisons (these knobs and the 10% deep drawdown) ignore float noise like the FTD gain test.
- ftd-detector: an index that closes at a new high resumes the uptrend without an FTD, as IBD does (new state `UPTREND_RESUMED`; `market_status` confirmed_uptrend or uptrend_under_pressure; `cycle.resumed_without_ftd`; no FTD event). The reference is the new tuning knob `FTD_RESUME_NEW_HIGH`, read when an index is analyzed: `pre_correction_high` (default), `52w`, `replay_high` or `off`; an invalid value warns on stderr and uses the default, and the effective value is in `cycle.parameters`. All confirmed-phase rules apply except the FTD-day-low cause. get_market_state, the quality score ("No FTD"), the report and stanley-druckenmiller (base 80, no FTD extras) handle the new state. The default is uncalibrated. Same-bar priority in an attempt: undercut, then FTD, then the Day-11 expiry, then resume (the resumed reference includes today's low); `replay_high` is the highest valid close across gap restarts; a failed FTD on the other index never overrides a resumed uptrend; the monitor guidance for a resumed uptrend no longer says "No Follow-Through Day detected. Stay defensive."
- ftd-detector: an FTD is valid on Day 4-10 only. An attempt with no FTD by Day 10 expires on the bar that would be Day 11 (RALLY_FAILED, "No follow-through day by Day 10") and a new segment starts at that bar (inclusive), so the next count's reference low is the lowest low from the expiry bar through its Day 1 (keeping the expired attempt's reference lost the October 2019 Nasdaq FTD). The expiry bar is never Day 1; while not armed a bar can only arm the count (a down close arms it), so Day 1 is never the arming bar; `reset_count` counts expiries; new output field `rally_attempt.end_reason` (`undercut` or `expired`) and RALLY_FAILED report guidance names which. The `ftd.late` output field and the "late FTD" report text of earlier drafts are removed. IBD's note that most FTDs come on days 4-7 and some later is cited as unverified.
- ftd-detector: the state machine is now a stateful forward replay of the whole history (500 sessions are fetched, up from 80) instead of a 60-session recompute. The old rule read any down close during a correction as the newest swing low and reset the rally count, replaced the anchor with a higher low in the same correction, failed a rally on the Day 1 intraday low, and dropped a confirmed FTD 40-60 sessions later. Now the rally low (the lowest intraday low of the pullback segment the attempt started from, frozen on Day 1; a segment restarts at a failure and an FTD invalidation, so it is not the lowest low of the whole correction) is the failure level for the attempt and after the FTD, a closing low only moves on a new lower close, the latest bar is never a confirmed low, a new closing low with an upper-half close can be Day 1, and an FTD leaves a correction (a new high also does, see the resume entry). New additive `market_status` (`confirmed_uptrend`, `uptrend_under_pressure`, `market_in_correction`, or null) per index and combined, `cycle` and `rally_attempt.reference_low`; legacy `state` values are unchanged and NO_SIGNAL now means warm-up, data gap or insufficient data. A confirmed uptrend goes under pressure at 5+ distribution days, a close below the 50-session SMA once the index has closed above it since the FTD (an FTD normally comes from below it), or the first close below the FTD-day low, returns to confirmed without a new FTD, and ends at 6+ distribution days with a close below the 50-session SMA (even if the SMA was never regained; the 21-session EMA trigger fired on shallow pullbacks and kept rising markets in correction, and the EMA is removed, so `cycle.indicators.ema21` and `cycle.indicators.ema21_available` are removed from the output) or a 10% drawdown from the post-FTD peak (unverified, uncalibrated model choices). A bad close, volume or low restarts the replay after it when 60 clean sessions remain, otherwise the index reports a data gap. Rebuilt `skill-packages/ftd-detector.skill`.
